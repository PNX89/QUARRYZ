Three idioms for one definition:
  clickhouse  argMax(value, (released, version))
  duckdb      row_number() over (order by released desc, version desc)
  postgres    distinct on (period) ... order by released desc, version desc

--- as at 2023-08 ---
667 periods compared, 0 disagreements
2021 reads -28039 in all three
0 periods where the release-date tie decided the answer

--- as at 2016-01 ---
306 periods compared, 0 disagreements
2021 reads absent in all three
14 periods where the release-date tie decided the answer

THE TIE IS THE POINT. This series has release dates carrying two versions with
different values. argMax(value, released) has no defined answer there, and at
2016-01 it returns the superseded one for 14 periods. At 2023-08 it
returns the right answer for all of them, because later releases have
superseded every tied period: a query that is wrong is right almost always.
